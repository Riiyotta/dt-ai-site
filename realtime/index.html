Why Realtime

Best of both worlds

Realtime is faster than Wire, configurable like ACH.

Faster than Wire, and more affordable, too.

Send or request funds. Realtime does both.

API request to settlement in under a second.

Settle any time, any day. What Bank holiday?

Why DT&AI

Fed at the speed of Now

With a direct connection to the Federal Reserve, DT&AI offers the full potential of FedNow's capabilities.

Create a Realtime transfer

curl 'https://api.dtai.com/transfers/realtime' \
    -u :live_1vWeqakwT2N7NYRtZkUPSBt4scn \
    -d counterparty_id=cpty_1vWeqakwT2N7NYRtZkUPSBt4scn \
    -d bank_account_id=bacc_1vWeqakwT2N7NYRtZkUPSBt4scn \
    -d amount=2241 \
    -d currency_code=USD \
    -d description="Lunch"
